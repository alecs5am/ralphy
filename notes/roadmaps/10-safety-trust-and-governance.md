# Safety, trust, and governance roadmap

> **Status:** roadmap source
> **Filed:** 2026-06-16
> **Folder:** roadmaps
> **Related issues:** #422, #442, #443, #444

## Purpose

Keep high-volume media production accountable. Safety and trust are not only
policy concerns. They also protect users from wasted spend, false claims, bad
brand usage, broken references, and outputs that cannot be published.

## Target capabilities

- Reference-required gate for real entities, products, likeness, and IP.
- Claim and proof handling.
- Brand fidelity and misuse warnings.
- Consent and provenance logs.
- Spend and approval governance.
- Platform policy checks where available.
- Safe bypasses with reasons.

## Workstreams

### Reference and likeness governance

Issue families:

- Reference-required classifier expansion.
- Likeness consent artifact.
- Brand product reference policy.

### Claims and compliance

Issue families:

- Claim extraction from brief and research.
- Proof requirement flags.
- Risky category detection.
- Claim-safe copy generation.
- Eval gate for unsupported claims.
- Distribution pack claim notes.

### Brand and product trust

Issue families:

- Product fidelity scoring.
- Logo use validation.
- Color and packaging consistency.
- Brand voice consistency.
- Product availability and feature caveats.
- Marketplace listing constraints.

### Governance logs

Issue families:

- Approval ledger improvements.
- Consent log schema.
- Bypass reason report.
- Provenance completeness gate.
- Spend anomaly warning.
- Audit summary in project postmortem.

## Acceptance ladder

1. Required reference checks block obvious real-entity gaps.
2. Claims are extracted and marked as proved, user-provided, or risky.
3. Distribution packs carry claim and provenance notes.
4. User bypasses are explicit and visible in postmortem.
5. Brand/product fidelity gates influence readiness.
6. Large batch runs summarize trust and governance exceptions.
