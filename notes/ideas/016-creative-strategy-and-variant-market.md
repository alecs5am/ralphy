# Creative strategy and variant market

> **Status:** idea
> **Filed:** 2026-06-15
> **Folder:** ideas

## Context

The content-farm goal is not one perfect output. It is many controlled attempts across hooks, personas, styles, CTAs, first frames, music beds, and platforms, with a system for choosing winners.

## What

Add a creative strategy layer above generation. It defines hypotheses, audience segments, offers, objections, angles, hooks, and variant axes. The variant engine then runs a controlled market: generate A/B/C, evaluate against benchmarks and platform goals, pick champions, preserve losers with reasons, and feed results into future batches.

## Why it matters

Without strategy, batch generation is just volume. The ideal Ralphy should learn which creative directions win and allocate budget toward better variants.
