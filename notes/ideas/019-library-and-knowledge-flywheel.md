# Library and knowledge flywheel

> **Status:** idea
> **Filed:** 2026-06-15
> **Folder:** ideas

## Context

The ideal Ralphy should get better with every project. Today lessons are scattered across issues, postmortems, memory, guidelines, templates, Units, and library entities.

## What

Build a knowledge flywheel: successful Units become templates/styles/recipes/assets; failures become guideline updates, model warnings, or mode rules; benchmarks come from curated Units; user-local templates can graduate to the public library; community uploads can expand the ecosystem after validation.

## Why it matters

The content factory only compounds if production output becomes reusable production input. Otherwise every agent starts from scratch.

## Notes

- Related issues: #113, #417, #420, #425, #447, #448, #067.
- Keep the route explicit: memory for durable preferences/failure rules, guidelines for prompt craft, templates for repeatable structure, Units for finished examples.
- Avoid auto-learning without review; bad lessons are worse than missing lessons.
