# Captions

## Shape per platform

| Platform | Shape | Hashtags | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | one hook line, one sentence | 3 to 5 | 2,200 characters |
| Instagram Reels | 2 to 4 sentences in the account voice, a soft call to action | 10 to 15 | 2,200 |
| YouTube Shorts | the first line is the title, at most ~40 characters reads fully (100 max) | few or none | 5,000 description |
| X | one line | 0 to 2 | 280 |

A caption over the limit fails the upload.

## Grounding

Read before writing:

- `notes.md` and the composition text: what the video says and shows;
- the reference's own caption and on-screen text (`refs/<slug>/source.json` `description`,
  `breakdown.md`): the hook that worked there is the best starting point;
- the account's voice in `account.md`.

A caption that could sit under any video is wrong.

## Language and voice

- The post language is the audience's language, not the chat's. A German audience gets German even
  when the chat is Russian.
- Match the niche register: meme formats talk like memes, a brand talks like the brand.
- The CTA keyword in the caption matches the one in the video exactly.

## Hashtags

- Take them from the niche's current top videos (`ralphy trends <niche>`, their captions), not from
  memory. Order: niche tags, format tags, one or two broad tags.
- Tags rot in about a month; refresh from current top posts before a series.
- No unrelated trending tags.

## Credits

Stock or borrowed material that requires attribution: put the credit line in the caption and note
the source in `notes.md`.
