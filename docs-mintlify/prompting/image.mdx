Image prompts in Ralphy follow a 6-part skeleton. Every part is doing work; missing any one of them is what makes the difference between an asset you ship and a $0.20 retry. This page is the skeleton plus six worked examples — selfie rant, broadcast caught-on-TV, Old Spice VFX, photoreal portrait, brainrot character, brand product hero. Read [Philosophy](/prompting/philosophy) first if you haven't.

## The 6-part skeleton

```text
1. Subject       — who/what is in frame, with identity tokens.
2. Framing       — how close, what angle, what aspect ratio.
3. Lens          — specific real camera + prime lens.
4. Lighting      — single source, direction, time of day.
5. Film / stock  — Kodak Portra 400, Cinestill 800T, digital no-grade.
6. Defect noise  — pores, grain, asymmetry, flyaway hair.
```

Two parts are non-obvious: the **lens** has to be a real specific lens (not "DSLR"), and the **defect noise** has to be explicit because the model's default is poreless and symmetric. Skip either and the output reads as AI.

A 7th implicit part — **anti-slop negatives** — appends to every photoreal-human gen. See [Philosophy](/prompting/philosophy#the-anti-ai-slop-block) for the block.

## Worked examples

### 1. Selfie rant (UGC creator monologue, 9:16)

```text
Alex, 32, dark brown hair short beard navy hoodie, mid-sentence with slight
eyebrow raise.

Selfie distance, eye-level, vertical 9:16, head-and-shoulders, slight handheld
tilt.

Sony FX3 + Sigma 35mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field.

Soft window light from screen-left, single source ambient indoor, late morning.

Kodak Portra 400 register, naturalistic NOT glossy, candid not staged.

Visible skin pores, flyaway hair at the temple, slight facial asymmetry,
imperceptible film grain.
```

- **Subject** is restated with identity tokens (age, hair, wardrobe) and a specific micro-expression. Restating identity inline beats relying on `--ref` alone — gemini drifts ~30% after 3+ unrefed gens.
- **Framing** locks the aspect ratio and the distance. "Selfie distance" is more reliable than "close-up" for selfie POV.
- **Lens** — `Sigma 35mm f/1.4` is the lens for selfie UGC. 50mm reads as portraiture; 85mm as fashion. 35mm is right.
- **Film stock** is the highest-impact token. Replace `Portra 400` and the whole register shifts.

### 2. Broadcast caught-on-TV (1:1 square)

Per the broadcast-realism rule: caught-on-TV trends default to 1:1 square, not 9:16. Real broadcast cameras shoot 16:9, and a 9:16 portrait read of a stadium reads as AI. The 1:1 crop convincingly fits the 16:9 broadcast geometry inside a portrait feed canvas.

```text
A flushed marathon runner mid-interview, sweat at the temples, microphone barely
in frame at the lower-left.

1:1 square, ENG broadcast camera cropped from 16:9, eye-level, slight handheld
jitter, subject filling 70% of frame.

Sony FX9 + 24-105mm zoom at 50mm, deep depth of field.

Stadium overhead mixed color temperature, key from camera-front, weak fill,
slight backlight from sun.

1080i broadcast TV color science, slight motion blur, NOT cinematic, NOT
color-graded.

Skin glistening from sweat, visible compression artifacts, slight banding in
gradients, broadcast-cam noise.
```

- **Aspect ratio is the load-bearing decision.** This is the broadcast-realism = square rule applied verbatim.
- **`Sony FX9`** + `1080i broadcast TV` — corpus-specific. "Broadcast TV" alone is too generic; `1080i` flags the interlaced source the model has seen in actual sports footage.
- **Defect noise here is broadcast-noise**, not film grain — compression artifacts, slight banding. Match the defect to the medium.

### 3. Old Spice deliberate VFX (intentionally fake creature)

Per the deliberate-prop-VFX rule: for "obviously-fake-on-purpose" creatures, all 5 cues are required — matte rubber, kelly-green-not-natural, visible seams, stiff posture, Muppet googly eyes. Skip any cue and it reads as AI failure instead of intentional craft.

```text
A 4-foot-tall matte-rubber sea creature in a hotel bathrobe, kelly-green color
(not seafoam, not lime — specifically kelly-green), Muppet-style googly eyes
with visible black plastic, stiff puppet posture, visible latex seams along
the back of the neck.

Wide three-quarter angle, full body in frame, vertical 9:16.

ARRI Alexa Mini + Zeiss Master Prime 32mm, high-key commercial light.

Hard fill from camera-front, soft kicker from screen-right, bright pastel
backdrop.

Bright pastel high-key commercial register, Old Spice DNA, saturated
NOT desaturated, NOT moody, NOT chiaroscuro.

Visible foam-latex texture, rubber-skin sheen, stiff joint articulation
visible.
```

- **`kelly-green (not seafoam, not lime)`** — naming the wrong shades is what locks the right one. Color words are ambiguous; eliminating the near-misses works.
- **`Bright pastel high-key commercial`** — the Old Spice register. The agent default of `cinematic` would pull it toward Tom Ford; the explicit "NOT moody, NOT chiaroscuro" negations close that door.
- This is one of the rare cases where the anti-slop philosophy *inverts*: glamour register is correct here because the brand DNA is glamour-as-comedy. See [Old Spice cookbook](/cookbook/oldspice-vfx).

### 4. Photoreal portrait (persona master)

This is the canonical photoreal-human anchor. The 5-cue checklist all 5 cues, anti-slop negatives default-on, identity restated inline.

```text
Black man, late 20s, soft warm features, shoulder-length thin natural locs,
thin gold wire-rim eyeglasses, faded grey crewneck.

Three-quarter portrait, eye-level, vertical 9:16, head and upper torso.

Leica M11 + Summicron 50mm f/2, f/2.8, shallow depth of field.

Open shade midday, soft single-source ambient, no fill.

Kodak Portra 400 register, hyperreal NOT glossy, naturalistic candid not staged.

Visible skin pores, slight uneven beard density, flyaway hair on one temple,
slight facial asymmetry, faint film grain.

Avoid: NO glossy beauty-filter skin, NO enlarged anime-eyes, NO smoothed
jawline-reshape, NO airbrush, NO uniform porelessness, NO catchlight-bomb,
NO Tom-Ford chiaroscuro, NO TV-commercial register, NO Apple-keynote rim-light.
```

- **Identity tokens are absurdly specific.** "Shoulder-length thin natural locs" beats "long hair"; "thin gold wire-rim" beats "glasses". Specificity holds across regens.
- **The two verbatim phrases** — `hyperreal NOT glossy` and `naturalistic candid not staged` — are both present.
- **The full anti-slop block is appended.** Per the playbook, default-on for every photoreal-human gen.

Read [Photoreal humans cookbook](/cookbook/photoreal-humans) for the full anatomy-checklist follow-up and locked-ref discipline.

### 5. Brainrot character (from the asset pool)

For named brainrot characters (Tralalero Tralala, Bombardiro Crocodilo, etc.), pull the reference from the [`ralphy-assets`](https://github.com/alecs5am/ralphy-assets) pool instead of describing from scratch. The model can't fabricate a recognizable named character without drift. See [`assets-catalog`](/concepts/asset-catalog).

```bash
# Check what the pool has before describing anything.
ralphy assets list --kind italian-brainrot-characters
# → tralalero-tralala, bombardiro-crocodilo, brr-brr-patapim, ...

# Pull a ref into the project.
ralphy assets pull-pool italian-brainrot-characters/tralalero-tralala \
  --install brainrot-shoe-001
```

Then the prompt becomes a positioning / scene description with the pulled ref as `--ref`:

```text
Tralalero Tralala (the blue shark with running shoes — see ref image), mid-stride
running through a bright tropical beach, three-quarter back view.

Wide angle, low camera, vertical 9:16, full body in frame.

Whimsical 3D illustration register matching the reference, saturated tropical
palette.

Hard sun, deep shadows, slight chromatic aberration.

Brainrot meme aesthetic, NOT photoreal, NOT cinematic.
```

If the asset doesn't exist in the pool, propose adding it rather than improvising. Per `AGENTS.md` invariant #12.

### 6. Brand product hero

The product is the subject. Glamour register is correct. The 5-cue photoreal checklist does not apply here; the brand-fidelity rules do.

```text
A 250ml Old Spice body wash bottle, white plastic with red and navy label,
embossed logo legible, droplet of water on the cap.

Macro close-up, centered, vertical 9:16, locked tripod, no movement.

Canon R5 + RF 100mm f/2.8L Macro, f/8, deep depth of field.

Single hard rim from screen-left, soft fill from camera-front, color-graded
teal/orange commercial.

Hyper-commercial CGI register, color-graded, polished.

Catchlight on the bottle curve, micro-droplets on label, subtle reflection on
glossy backdrop.

Avoid: no logo distortion, no fake branding, no unauthorized brand modification.
```

- **Brand fidelity negatives** are the key here. `no logo distortion, no fake branding` keeps gemini-3-pro from "improving" the trademarked logo. The logo only comes from the `--ref`; we don't generate it.
- For embedded text on labels (kanji buttons, LED digits, brand glyphs), prefer `openai/gpt-5.4-image-2` — gemini-3-pro smudges small typography (postmortem: flipper).

## Per-model picks

| Use case | Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Default — multi-ref / character consistency | `google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview` | Best at holding face/wardrobe/product identity across 2-3 refs. Tolerates ≥4 concurrent calls. ~$0.15/image. Default since 2026-05-20 per `MODELS.md` (re-flip from the brief gpt-5.4-image-2 default). |
| Premium typography / label accuracy | `openai/gpt-5.4-image-2` | Best typography on labels, cleanest hero product shots where the wordmark must read crisp. Caps at 1 concurrent — serialize batches. ~$0.20/image. |
| Budget iteration | `openai/gpt-5-image-mini` | ~$0.08, fine for exploration. |
| Cheapest smoke-test | `google/gemini-2.5-flash-image` | ~$0.02, visible quality dip. |

Pick from `MODELS.md` — never from memory.

<Warning>
`openai/gpt-5.4-image-2` has an OpenRouter concurrent-call cap of 1. Image batches >1 in parallel return `403 "Key limit exceeded (total limit)"`. Either run at `--concurrency 1` or swap to `google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview` which tolerates ≥4 parallel. See `MODELS.md` Lesson 9.
</Warning>

## Aspect ratio decisions

The `--size` flag is a prompt-level hint, not an enforced constraint. Image models round to internal natural buckets: 1024² for 1:1, ~768×1376 for 9:16, ~1280×720 for 16:9. You will not get pixel-exact 1080×1920 — downstream Remotion handles scale-to-cover.

| Aspect | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | Selfie POV, TikTok native, single-character UGC | Default |
| 1:1 | Broadcast realism, caught-on-TV, sports/news | Per the broadcast-square rule — never 9:16 for these |
| 16:9 | YouTube, cinema reference, wide establishing | Use sparingly in feed compositions |

## Reference images: when to attach

The `--ref` flag accepts URL, local path, or `data:` URI. Local paths auto-convert to `data:` URI in-process — no upload step.

**Always attach a ref when:**

- A named real entity is in the brief (person / brand / IP). The ref-required gate fires per [References](/concepts/references).
- The project has ≥2 scenes sharing a setting — generate the location plate first, pass it as `--ref` on every scene.
- The project has a persona — generate the persona master once, pass it as `--ref` on every downstream gen. Gemini's identity drift after 3+ unrefed gens is ~30%.

**Multi-ref pattern (gemini-3-pro):**

```bash
ralphy generate image --project <id> --slot scene-03-bg \
  --prompt "<scene description>" \
  --ref workspace/projects/<id>/assets/uploaded/persona-master.png \
  --ref workspace/projects/<id>/assets/uploaded/location-plate.png \
  --ref workspace/projects/<id>/assets/uploaded/product-ref.jpg
```

Both `gpt-5.4-image-2` and `gemini-3-pro-image-preview` accept image inputs; gemini is materially stronger at multi-ref consistency when you pass 2+ refs.

## CLI surface

```bash
# Generate an image — default model is the registry default in MODELS.md.
ralphy generate image --project <id> --slot scene-01-bg \
  --prompt "<text>" \
  [--ref <url-or-path> ...] \
  [--model <id>] \
  [--size 1080x1920] \
  [--negative "<text>"]

# Regen the same slot — auto-versions to .v2, .v3, etc. Append-only.
ralphy generate image --project <id> --slot scene-01-bg --prompt "<revised>"

# Cost preview without spending credits.
ralphy generate image --project <id> --slot scene-01-bg --prompt "<text>" --dry-run
```

Every call is logged to `workspace/projects/<id>/logs/generations.jsonl` with prompt, model, latency, and cost. See [Generation log](/concepts/generation-log).
