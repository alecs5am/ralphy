---
title: "Brand launch with refs"
description: "Multi-scene product launch. Lock product and persona masters first; pass both as --ref on every gen."
---

A 30-second 9:16 multi-scene brand product launch. Six scenes, one product, one
spokesperson — the product is a recognizable object the model cannot fabricate
without a reference, and the spokesperson appears in every scene. The recipe is
identity-lock by `--ref` chaining: generate the product master and the persona
master FIRST, then pass both as `--ref` on every downstream scene gen. Skip this
step and the product packaging drifts across scenes, the spokesperson grows a
different jawline by scene-04, and the launch reads as six unrelated stock clips.

## Brief

> "Launch our new matte black cordless headphones. 30s, 9:16, six scenes. Product
> reveal, hands picking up, woman in her 30s wearing them at a kitchen counter,
> close-up on the ear cup, walking shot, final wordmark over hero product shot.
> The product photo and the spokesperson photo are attached."

## Stack

- **Template:** [`multi-scene-product-launch`](https://github.com/alecs5am/ralphy/tree/main/templates/dtc-commerce/multi-scene-product-launch) (`vibe-style`, dtc-commerce)
- **Image anchors:** `openai/gpt-5.4-image-2` for the product master (kept on gpt-5.4-image-2 because the headphones wordmark must read crisp — gemini drifts on small label type); `google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview` (the current default since 2026-05-20) for the persona master and all scene anchors (multi-ref consistency)
- **Video:** `kwaivgi/kling-v3.0-pro` motion-only — no `--audio`, VO is ElevenLabs over the cut
- **VO:** ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2` over the full cut
- **Music:** `elevenlabs/music_v1` — 30s instrumental bed
- **Refs needed:** product photo + spokesperson photo (gate FIRES — this is a real branded product and a real model)
- **Runtime:** ~14 min cold-start
- **Cost:** ~$8.20 (8 images + 6× 5s Kling clips + 1 VO + 1 music)

## Walkthrough

<Steps>

<Step title="Reference gate — the brief names a real product and a real person">
The brief identifies a real brand product (the cordless headphones) and a real
person (the user's spokesperson). Both fire the gate.

```bash
ralphy ref check --text "Launch our new matte black cordless headphones, woman in her 30s spokesperson"
# → { "needsReference": true, "reasons": ["product-named", "persona-named-or-implied"] }
```

The agent refuses to generate without refs:

> "I need a product photo and a spokesperson photo before I generate. Drop both,
> or switch the spokesperson to a generic archetype."
</Step>

<Step title="Register the refs">
Save both photos under `assets/uploaded/` and log them. The CLI auto-logs via
`logUserAsset` when the upload happens through `ralphy project log-asset`.

```bash
ralphy project log-asset headphones-launch-001 \
  --kind photo --source ~/Downloads/product.png --purpose product-ref
ralphy project log-asset headphones-launch-001 \
  --kind photo --source ~/Downloads/spokesperson.png --purpose persona-ref
```
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the product master (anchor #1)">
The product master is the load-bearing identity asset. `gpt-5.4-image-2` holds
small typography (logo, button labels, port markings) across redos better than
gemini, so the product master is one of the few slots where we override the
gemini default.

```bash
ralphy generate image \
  --project headphones-launch-001 \
  --slot product-master \
  --model openai/gpt-5.4-image-2 \
  --ref workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/assets/uploaded/product-ref.png \
  --size 1024x1024 \
  --prompt "Studio product shot of the matte black cordless headphones (see ref — match the exact silhouette, ear cup curve, headband stitching, control buttons, and any visible wordmark). Plain off-white seamless background, soft overhead key from screen-right, naturalistic NOT glossy product-shot register, no rim-light explosion. Hyperreal, no logo distortion, no fake branding."
# → workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/assets/image/product-master.png
```

Self-check against the user's ref. If the wordmark is wrong, regen once with
the wordmark explicitly described. If still wrong, ask the user for a tighter
crop of the wordmark.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the persona master (anchor #2)">
One spokesperson, one master frame, eye-level neutral expression. Use the
photoreal-humans register from `art-director/photoreal-humans.md`.

```bash
ralphy generate image \
  --project headphones-launch-001 \
  --slot persona-master \
  --model google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview \
  --ref workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/assets/uploaded/persona-ref.png \
  --size 1024x1024 \
  --prompt "Woman in her early 30s (see ref — match face, hair color and length, eye color, skin tone, freckles if visible). Soft neutral expression, three-quarter front, looking past the lens. Sony A7 IV + Sigma 85mm f/1.8 ART, Kodak Portra 400, soft window light from screen-left, hyperreal NOT glossy, naturalistic candid not staged. NO glossy beauty-filter skin, NO enlarged anime-eyes, NO smoothed jawline, NO airbrush, NO Tom-Ford chiaroscuro."
```

Run the anatomy + identity check before moving on. The persona master is
referenced on every downstream scene, so a flawed master compounds.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the storyboard scene anchors (one at a time)">
Six scenes. Generate scene-01 first, surface to the user, wait for approval,
then proceed. Batching all six upfront before the first one is approved is the
anti-pattern this recipe exists to prevent.

```bash
ralphy generate image \
  --project headphones-launch-001 \
  --slot scene-01-bg \
  --model google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview \
  --ref workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/assets/image/product-master.png \
  --ref workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/assets/image/persona-master.png \
  --size 1080x1920 \
  --prompt "Hero reveal shot. The matte black cordless headphones (see ref-1) sit on a sun-washed light-oak kitchen counter, soft morning light from a window screen-right, low three-quarter angle. The woman from ref-2 is out of frame for this shot. Hyperreal NOT cinematic, naturalistic editorial product register."
```

Repeat for scene-02 through scene-06. Every gen passes BOTH masters as `--ref`,
even on scenes where one of them is "out of frame" — the model uses the ref to
hold the wardrobe / setting cues that flowed from the master.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the six Kling motion clips">
5s each, motion-only (no `--audio`). VO is a separate ElevenLabs pass over the
full cut, which gives you a single continuous narrator track instead of six
disjointed mid-clip voices.

```bash
ralphy generate video \
  --project headphones-launch-001 \
  --slot scene-01-vid \
  --model kwaivgi/kling-v3.0-pro \
  --image workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/assets/image/scene-01-bg.png \
  --duration 5 \
  --prompt "Slow push-in on the headphones on the counter. Soft handheld micro-jitter. Morning light shifts subtly across the surface. NO music, NO speech."
# repeat for scene-02 through scene-06
```

Each clip's start frame must show a distinct beat from the end frame — gesture,
camera position, or light shift. Identical start/end frames produce a static
clip that reads as a stale photo.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate VO + music + render">
```bash
ralphy generate voiceover \
  --project headphones-launch-001 \
  --slot full-vo \
  --voice <voice-id> \
  --text "<the 30s narration script>"

ralphy generate music \
  --project headphones-launch-001 \
  --slot bed-01 \
  --duration 32 \
  --prompt "Warm minimalist instrumental, 90 BPM, light analog synth pad, soft kick on the downbeat, no vocals, no lead melody, premium hardware-launch register"

ralphy generate captions --project headphones-launch-001 --audio workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/assets/voiceover/full-vo.mp3
ralphy render headphones-launch-001
# → workspace/projects/headphones-launch-001/render/final.mp4 (30.0s, 9:16)
```
</Step>

</Steps>

## Why this works

Identity drift across multi-scene projects is the dominant failure mode of
AI-generated brand work. After three unrefed gens, gemini's identity drift is
around 30% on faces and packaging; by scene-06 the spokesperson has a different
jawline and the headphones have grown an extra port. The fix is locked refs on
every gen, even when intuition says "the model remembers what we just generated".
It doesn't — every generation is independent, and the only durable identity
signal is the ref payload on each call.

`gpt-5.4-image-2` for the product master (small typography hold — the one slot
where we override the gemini default) and `gemini-3-pro-image-preview` for the
persona master + every scene anchor (multi-ref consistency across 30+ gens) is
the canonical pairing. The product master goes into every
downstream call regardless of whether the headphones are visible in the frame —
the ref carries the brand register (color, materiality, lighting style) that
should propagate through the wardrobe and setting choices.

The anchor order matters. Generate the product master first, the persona master
second, then scenes one at a time. Skipping the master-first step and going
straight to scene anchors is the highest-leverage mistake in this recipe —
every scene anchor then carries an inconsistent "implicit master" that gemini
inferred from the scene prompt, and continuity collapses by scene-04.

Kling motion-only with ElevenLabs VO over the cut beats Kling `--audio` per-clip
for multi-scene launches. One continuous narrator track in the editor reads as
one ad; six per-clip `--audio` lines read as six fragmented voice takes. Use
`--audio` only when a single clip has on-camera dialogue.

## Variations

- **Fewer scenes.** Three-scene launch (hero, demo, wordmark) cuts cost to ~$4
  and runtime to ~7 min. Still requires the master-first discipline.
- **Russian / non-English VO.** Same recipe — Kling clips stay motion-only,
  ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2` covers any language.
- **No spokesperson.** Drop the persona master. The product master alone carries
  the launch if every scene is a product-led shot. The reference gate still
  fires on the branded product.
- **Audio on one scene only.** If the spokesperson has one on-camera line in
  scene-03, generate that scene with Kling `--audio` and keep the rest
  motion-only. The composition stitches the on-camera line into the narrator
  track.
- **Aspect.** 1:1 for IG feed, 16:9 for YouTube hero, 9:16 for TikTok / Reels.
  The product master is square — round into the target aspect at the scene
  anchor stage.

## Pitfalls

<Warning>
The reference-required gate is not a warning — it is a refusal. Without product
and persona photos, the agent will not generate the masters. Override only via
`--no-ref-consent "<reason>"` on the specific failing call; expect quality
degradation when overridden.
</Warning>

<Warning>
Do not generate all six scene anchors before surfacing scene-01 to the user.
Locking in six anchors against a flawed master burns ~$4 across the batch and
roughly 45 min of regen time. One at a time, with checkpoints.
</Warning>

<Warning>
Do not drop the product master from a scene gen "because the headphones aren't
visible". Every scene gen carries both masters as `--ref`, every time. The
master propagates the brand register, not just the literal object.
</Warning>
