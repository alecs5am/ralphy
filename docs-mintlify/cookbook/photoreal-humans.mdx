A two-person deadpan-philosophical sketch on a couch — two heads tipped back,
eyes-up, almost-whispered half-rhetorical questions. The aesthetic is candid
still-photo, not TV commercial. The recipe documents the photoreal-still register
that holds across multi-scene human work: Sony A7 IV body + Sigma prime lens +
specific f-stop + Kodak Portra 400 film stock + ambient single-source light +
"hyperreal NOT glossy, naturalistic candid not staged" verbatim + identity
restating inline. All five cues required. Drop one and the model defaults to
beauty-filter / TV-commercial slop.

## Brief

> "Two friends on a tan corduroy couch, late-20s, heads tipped back on the back
> cushion looking up at the ceiling, deadpan delivery, 'do you think the spoons in
> our drawer are aware of each other' kind of vibe. 20s, English VO, photoreal not
> glossy."

## Stack

- **Template:** [`noski-deadpan-2hander`](https://github.com/alecs5am/ralphy/tree/main/templates/creator-lifestyle/noski-deadpan-2hander) (`vibe-style`, creator-lifestyle)
- **Image anchors:** `google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview` (default since 2026-05-20, nano-banana-pro lineage) for every still in the project — location plate, persona masters, and scene anchors. Multi-ref consistency is the entire point of this recipe; gpt-5.4-image-2 only buys you typography fidelity, which a candid-couch shot doesn't need.
- **Video:** `kwaivgi/kling-v3.0-pro --audio` — two 10s i2v clips with native lipsync
- **Refs needed:** none (generic archetypes, no real persons)
- **Runtime:** ~10 min cold-start
- **Cost:** ~$3.10 (4 images + 2× 10s Kling pro)

<Step title="Generate the location-master plate FIRST">

```bash
ralphy generate image \
  --project spoon-awareness-001 \
  --slot location-plate \
  --model google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview \
  --size 1024x1024 \

Sony A7 IV + Sigma 35mm f/2.8 ART, Kodak Portra 400, soft natural single-source ambient, hyperreal NOT glossy, naturalistic candid not staged. Run the anatomy check from the photoreal-humans playbook: hips on seat, back
against cushion, knees forward, head-body neck angle plausible, 2 arms + 2 legs

explicitly negated.

<Step title="Generate scene anchors with all three refs">
Pass location plate + both persona masters on every scene anchor. Gemini holds
three refs cleanly when they each carry orthogonal identity signal (setting,
character A, character B).

```bash
ralphy generate image \
  --project spoon-awareness-001 \
  --slot scene-01-bg \
  --model google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview \
  --ref workspace/projects/spoon-awareness-001/assets/image/location-plate.png \
  --ref workspace/projects/spoon-awareness-001/assets/image/persona-a-master.png \
  --ref workspace/projects/spoon-awareness-001/assets/image/persona-b-master.png \
  --size 1080x1920 \
  --prompt "Top-down camera angle looking down at two late-20s friends sitting side by side on the tan corduroy couch (see ref-1). Persona A (see ref-2 — Black man, locs, gold wire-rims, cream sweater) on the left, persona B (see ref-3 — Asian-American woman, dark hair, charcoal cardigan) on the right. Both have heads fully tipped back on the back-cushion, eyes wide open looking up at the ceiling, soft natural expressions, mouths slightly parted ready to speak. Sony A7 IV + Sigma 35mm f/2.8 ART (top-down), Kodak Portra 400, soft window light from screen-left, hyperreal NOT glossy, naturalistic candid not staged. NO Tom-Ford chiaroscuro, NO TV-commercial register."
```

Top-down for head-back-eyes-up is the strongest geometry — three-quarter-front
plus head-back is physically conflicting and the model resolves by closing
the eyes or floating the heads. Use top-down here.
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the Kling clips">
Two 10s clips, both with `--audio`. The dialogue is split between the two
characters; Kling reads the `[VOICE A]` / `[VOICE B]` distinction.

## Why this works

The default model behavior on "photoreal human" prompts is TV-commercial
register — Tom-Ford chiaroscuro, marble + tuxedo, ARRI Alexa, Cooke S4 lens.
That register reads as AI-slop because the lighting and grading are too
controlled for what the audience expects from candid handheld content. The
still-photo register (Sony A7 IV, Sigma prime, Portra 400, single-source ambient)
is the candid-documentary fingerprint, and it pulls the model into a basin
trained on millions of editorial still photos rather than millions of perfume
commercials.

All five cues are required, validated across the noski-people-001,
venom-bodywash-001, and glitter-cream-001 projects. The single highest-impact
token is the film stock — "Kodak Portra 400" shifts the model out of
beauty-filter / cinema-LUT territory more aggressively than any other single
addition. The phrases "hyperreal NOT glossy" and "naturalistic candid not
staged" are verbatim the strongest anti-AI-slop tokens in the gemini-3-pro
vocabulary. The anti-AI-slop block (`NO glossy beauty-filter skin, NO enlarged
anime-eyes, NO smoothed jawline-reshape, NO airbrush…`) goes into every gen by
default — user feedback "too AI-perfectly-perfect" traces directly to skipping
this block.

`gemini-3-pro-image-preview` carries every still in this recipe — single-portrait
persona masters, the location plate, and scene anchors that need to hold three
refs at once. Multi-ref consistency is gemini's strength, and a candid couch
shot has no labels to worry about. Anchor order: location plate first, then
personas, then scene anchors with all three refs. Skipping the location plate
is the single highest-leverage failure mode in multi-scene human work — it costs
~$4.50 + 45 min on a 5-scene project.

`bytedance/seedance-2.0` is off the table for photoreal-human i2v — the privacy
filter rejects AI-generated human anchors with `InputImageSensitiveContentDetected`.
Kling `--audio` for EN, Kling motion-only + ElevenLabs for everything else.

## Variations

- **Single character.** Drop persona-b-master and persona-b prompts. Keep the
  location plate. The 5-cue register is the same.
- **Three+ scenes.** Same masters propagate. Each new scene anchor passes the
  location plate + relevant personas. Gemini holds the masters cleanly through
  ~10 scene gens; past that, drift starts and you re-anchor.
- **Outdoor location.** Replace "soft window light from screen-left" with "open
  shade midday" or "evening interior with lamp". Keep the single-source phrasing —
  multi-source "studio" defaults to beauty-filter.
- **Russian dialogue.** Drop `--audio` on Kling. Generate motion-only, then
  ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2` for VO over the cut. Kling `--audio` slips
  outside English.
- **Less-formal register.** "Sony A7 IV + Sigma 35mm f/2.8 ART" can swap to
  "Leica M11 + Summicron 50mm" or "Canon R5 + RF 85mm f/1.2". Each pulls a
  slightly different look. Avoid generic "DSLR" — the model needs a specific
  body to commit.

## Pitfalls

<Warning>
All five cues are required: real camera + prime lens, real film stock, ambient
single-source light, "hyperreal NOT glossy + naturalistic candid not staged"
verbatim, and identity-restating inline. Three or four cues is not enough — the
model drifts into beauty-filter / TV-commercial register on the missing axis.
</Warning>

<Warning>
For head-fully-back-on-cushion + eyes-up, use top-down camera. Three-quarter-
front plus head-back is physically conflicting and the model resolves by closing
the eyes or floating the heads. Top-down has both eyes plainly visible and the
gaze direction is unambiguous.
</Warning>

<Warning>
Generate the location plate FIRST in any multi-scene project where ≥2 scenes
share a setting. Skipping it produces "different couch in every shot" and costs
roughly $5 + 45 min to recover.
</Warning>
