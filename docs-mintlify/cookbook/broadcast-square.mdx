A 15-second 1:1 square clip that looks like a TV broadcast someone phone-recorded
off a screen — sports sideline, news studio cut-in, audience cam mid-event. The
aesthetic trick is the aspect ratio. Real broadcast cameras shoot 16:9, so square
sits in the uncanny middle and reads as documentary capture; portrait reads as AI
generation. Kling carries the motion with `--audio` providing the ambient announcer
or crowd track.

## Brief

> "Make a 15s caught-on-TV moment of a baseball stadium just as the home team turns
> a triple play. Square, ambient stadium roar, announcer voice underneath, like
> someone filmed the bar TV with their phone."

## Stack

- **Template:** [`broadcast-caught-on-tv-square`](https://github.com/alecs5am/ralphy/tree/main/templates/entertainment-viral/broadcast-caught-on-tv-square) (`vibe-style`, entertainment-viral)
- **Image anchor:** `openai/gpt-5.4-image-2` — square 1024×1024, holds typography on chyron / scoreboard
- **Video:** `kwaivgi/kling-v3.0-pro --audio` — single 15s i2v clip with ambient announcer
- **Refs needed:** none for generic sport / news; reference-required gate fires if the brief names a specific team / broadcaster
- **Runtime:** ~5 min cold-start
- **Cost:** ~$2.30 (one square image + one 15s Kling pro clip)

## Walkthrough

<Steps>

<Step title="Check the reference gate">
"Baseball stadium, triple play" is generic. "Yankee Stadium, ninth-inning triple

```bash

# → { "needsReference": false, "reasons": [] }
```

If the brief had named a team, the agent would refuse without a reference or
ask the user for `--no-ref-consent "<reason>"` on the failing call.
</Step>

<Step title="Scaffold from the template">
```bash
ralphy template use broadcast-caught-on-tv-square \
  --project triple-play-001 \
  --name "Triple-play broadcast moment" \
  --brief "15s 1:1 caught-on-TV moment of a baseball triple play, ambient stadium roar + announcer"
```
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the square anchor frame">
The first frame is the load-bearing asset. Chyron text and scoreboard digits
read clean on `gpt-5.4-image-2`; gemini smudges small typography.

```bash
ralphy generate image \
  --project triple-play-001 \
  --slot scene-01-bg \
  --model openai/gpt-5.4-image-2 \
  --size 1024x1024 \
  --prompt "Square 1:1 broadcast freeze-frame as if someone is phone-recording a sports bar TV. Wide centerfield camera on a sunlit ballpark, base runners mid-stride, infielders turning a double play to first. Lower-third yellow chyron reads 'BOTTOM 9TH · 2 OUTS'. Scoreboard top-right shows 4-3 score. Slight TV moire pattern, soft phone-screen glare in the top-right corner, naturalistic broadcast color, hyperreal NOT cinematic, NO Tom-Ford chiaroscuro, NO Apple-keynote rim-light."
# → workspace/projects/triple-play-001/assets/image/scene-01-bg.png
```

If the chyron text comes out smudged on the first take, regenerate once with
explicit lower-third coordinates. If still smudged, redesign — drop the chyron
text and let the audio carry the moment (per `regeneration.md` one-retry rule).
</Step>

<Step title="Generate the Kling clip with ambient announcer">
The dialogue tag for caught-on-TV uses an off-screen announcer register, not
on-camera speech. Kling delivers ambient stadium roar + announcer voice when
`--audio` is on and the prompt frames the dialogue as off-screen.

```bash
ralphy generate video \
  --project triple-play-001 \
  --slot scene-01-vid \
  --model kwaivgi/kling-v3.0-pro \
  --image workspace/projects/triple-play-001/assets/image/scene-01-bg.png \
  --duration 15 \
  --audio \
  --prompt "Hold on the wide centerfield camera. Base runners commit, the shortstop fields the grounder cleanly and flips to second, the second baseman pivots and fires to first. Crowd builds from murmur to roar across the clip. Subtle TV camera handheld micro-jitter, no whip pans. [VOICE: off-screen TV play-by-play announcer, hyped American male mid-40s, English] He gets it on the ground, to second, on to first — TRIPLE PLAY, are you kidding me?! The stadium is on its feet! Ambient stadium roar swells under the announcer. NO music."
# → workspace/projects/triple-play-001/assets/video/scene-01-vid.mp4
```

The `NO music` clause is required — Kling's "broadcast moment" basin reaches
for stadium-anthem stings unless explicitly blocked.
</Step>

<Step title="Render">
The composition places the square clip on a 9:16 canvas with phone-screen
bezels above and below if the user wants to ship to TikTok, or full-frame 1:1
for IG / X.

```bash
ralphy render triple-play-001
# → workspace/projects/triple-play-001/render/final.mp4 (15.0s, 1:1)
```
</Step>

## Why this works

Real broadcast cameras shoot 16:9. When a phone records a TV screen, the visible
content sits in a 16:9 rectangle inside a portrait phone frame, with bezels and
glare around it. Square 1:1 captures the perceptual fingerprint of that recording
without committing to one bezel style — the eye reads it as "broadcast on a
display, captured incidentally", which is the documentary register. Portrait
9:16 reads as native-vertical, and native-vertical sports / news cuts only exist
on social — the moment it appears as a "broadcast", the AI tell becomes obvious.

`gpt-5.4-image-2` is the right anchor model because broadcast caught-on-TV
moments live or die on the chyron / scoreboard text. gemini-3-pro is stronger
on multi-ref consistency, but it smudges small typography (flipper, playdate
postmortems all hit this) — and there is no multi-ref demand here because the
recipe is one shot. gpt-5.4-image-2 holds the chyron through three redos.

Kling `--audio` delivers ambient announcer / crowd via the `[VOICE: off-screen]`
tag, and stays in EN where the model is clean. The 15s length is exactly the
Kling pro ceiling — enough to escalate from murmur to roar across the play.
Veo 3.1 would force a cut at 8s and lose the build.

## Variations

- **News studio cut-in.** Anchor frame is a news desk with breaking-news chyron;
  Kling motion is a hands-folded read-in. Same square aspect, same announcer tag.
- **Audience cam.** Anchor is a press-box wide on a packed arena; Kling motion is
  the crowd standing in waves. The announcer becomes the PA crackle.
- **Bezel composition.** Wrap the 1:1 in 9:16 phone-screen bezels in the editor
  if the destination is TikTok / Reels — keeps the "filmed off a TV" reading.
- **Brand override.** If the user wants a specific team / network, attach a
  reference logo, then add it as `--ref` on the image gen. The reference gate
  blocks the call without it.
- **Length.** Drop to 8s for a single-beat shot (just the play, no build). Above
  15s, Kling's continuity drops — split into two i2v clips with shared anchor.

## Pitfalls

<Warning>
Do not generate this in 9:16. The whole illusion depends on the aspect feeling
"filmed off a TV", and native-portrait broadcast does not exist. Portrait
output reads as AI generation even when the per-frame realism is correct.
</Warning>

<Warning>
Do not skip the `NO music` clause. Kling's broadcast basin reaches for
stadium-anthem stings or news-bumper music unless explicitly blocked, and once
the music is baked in you cannot remove it from the clip.
</Warning>

<Warning>
Real broadcaster names (`ESPN`, `Sky Sports`) fire the reference-required gate.
Attach a logo reference or stay generic. Don't override with `--no-ref-consent`
unless the user knows the typography will misrender.
</Warning>
